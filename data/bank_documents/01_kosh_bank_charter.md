# Kosh Bank Charter, Regulatory Governance & Customer Rights

## 1. Regulatory Status and Banking License
Kosh Bank Limited is a simulated Scheduled Commercial Bank governed by the regulatory framework of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. Kosh operates as a full-service, digital-first commercial banking institution authorized to conduct retail banking, corporate banking, foreign exchange operations, and clearinghouse transactions.

All banking operations adhere strictly to the guidelines, master circulars, and directives issued by the Reserve Bank of India, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), and the Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND).

## 2. Deposit Insurance (DICGC Coverage)
All deposits held with Kosh Bank—including savings bank accounts, current accounts, recurring deposits, and fixed deposits—are comprehensively insured by the **Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC)**, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Reserve Bank of India.
- **Insurance Coverage Limit**: Up to **₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs)** per individual depositor across principal and interest amounts held in the same capacity and same right across all branches.
- **Eligible Accounts**: Suvarna Premier Savings, Digital Smart Savings, Vriddhi Smart FDs, Flexi-Recurring Deposits, and Institutional Escrow Accounts.
- **Coverage Period**: Active from the date of account opening and continuously maintained without lapse.

## 3. RBI Charter of Customer Rights
Kosh Bank recognizes and upholds the five fundamental rights enshrined in the Reserve Bank of India's Charter of Customer Rights:
1. **Right to Fair Treatment**: Customers are treated courteously, fairly, and without discrimination based on gender, age, religion, caste, or disability in all interactions, branch services, and algorithmic digital recommendations.
2. **Right to Transparency, Fair and Honest Dealing**: All contracts, terms and conditions, interest calculation methodologies, tariff schedules, fees, and penalties are stated plainly without hidden charges or ambiguous clauses.
3. **Right to Suitability**: Kosh's digital and human advisors recommend financial products tailored strictly to the customer's financial status, stated risk tolerance, and verified requirements.
4. **Right to Privacy**: Personal, financial, and transactional data is treated with zero-trust security and end-to-end encryption. Kosh never sells or shares customer personal information with external marketers or third parties.
5. **Right to Grievance Redress and Compensation**: Customers have unhindered access to an independent, multi-tiered grievance redressal system and are compensated promptly for operational lapses or system errors as mandated by RBI timelines.

## 4. Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Banking Ombudsman
Kosh Bank is committed to resolving customer complaints transparently and swiftly:
- **Level 1 (Branch & AI Support)**: Complaints can be registered 24x7 via Mitra (Kosh Bank's AI banker), web portal, mobile application, or email at `grievance@kosh.bank`. Resolution timeline: **Within 48 to 72 hours**.
- **Level 2 (Principal Nodal Officer)**: If not satisfied with the Level 1 resolution within 7 working days, the customer may escalate to the Principal Nodal Officer at `nodal.officer@kosh.bank`.
- **Level 3 (Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme)**: If a complaint is not resolved within **30 days** of initial filing, or if the customer is dissatisfied with the final resolution, the customer has the statutory right to escalate the matter free of charge to the **RBI Integrated Ombudsman** via the CMS portal at `https://cms.rbi.org.in` or toll-free number 14448.

## 5. Security & Zero Customer Liability Policy
In full accordance with RBI Master Circular on Limiting Customer Liability in Unauthorized Electronic Banking Transactions:
- **Zero Liability**: A customer has **zero liability** where an unauthorized transaction occurs in the event of contributory fraud/negligence/deficiency on the part of the bank, or a third-party breach where the customer notifies Kosh within **three (3) working days** of receiving the transaction alert.
- **Limited Liability**: If the customer reports an unauthorized electronic transaction within **four (4) to seven (7) working days**, the maximum customer liability is capped at ₹10,000 for savings accounts and basic credit cards.
- **Instant Card Freezing**: Customers can instantly freeze or block debit and credit cards with a single toggle via the mobile app, web dashboard, or through Mitra, Kosh Bank's AI banker.
