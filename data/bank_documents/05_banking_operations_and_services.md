# Kosh Bank Operations, Services & Transaction Guidelines

## 1. Chequebook Services, Dispatch & Positive Pay System (PPS)
Kosh Bank provides automated and on-demand chequebook facilities for all active checking and savings account holders.

### Issuance Entitlement & Charges
- **Standard Annual Entitlement**: The first **25-leaf personalized chequebook** is issued **complimentary (free)** every financial year for each active savings account.
- **Additional Chequebooks**: Subsequent 25-leaf or 50-leaf chequebooks are charged at a nominal rate of **₹3 per leaf** + GST (e.g., ₹75 for 25 leaves, ₹150 for 50 leaves).
- **Dispatch Timeline**: Chequebooks are personalized, printed, and dispatched within **three (3) working days** from the request date via Speed Post or registered courier with real-time SMS and email tracking links.

### CTS-2010 Compliance & Clearing Timelines
- All Kosh cheques conform to **Cheque Truncation System (CTS-2010)** standards featuring void pantographs, chemical sensitivity, and magnetic ink character recognition (MICR) bands.
- **Clearing Timeline**: CTS-2010 clearing cycle operates on T+1 business day turnaround across all standard RBI clearing grid hubs (Western Grid - Mumbai, Northern Grid - New Delhi, Southern Grid - Chennai).

### Positive Pay System (PPS)
In compliance with the RBI directive on Positive Pay Mechanism for Cheques:
- Customers issuing high-value cheques must submit key verification details prior to presentment:
  - **Voluntary Threshold**: Strongly recommended for cheques of **₹50,000 and above**.
  - **Mandatory Threshold**: Required for all cheques of **₹5,00,000 and above**.
- Details submitted via PPS include: Cheque number, 6-digit cheque date, payee/beneficiary name, and exact amount.
- Cheques exceeding ₹5,00,000 without prior Positive Pay confirmation are flagged and held pending customer verification to protect depositors against forged alterations.

## 2. Electronic Fund Transfers: Limits, Availability & Timelines
Kosh supports round-the-clock digital payments with high throughput:

### Transfer Rails Comparison
| Payment Mode | Minimum Amount | Standard Daily Limit | Settlement Speed | Operating Hours | Transfer Fee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UPI** | ₹1 | ₹1,00,000 / day *(₹5 Lakh for hospitals/education)* | Instant (< 3 seconds) | 24x7x365 | ₹0 (Free) |
| **IMPS** | ₹1 | ₹5,00,000 / day | Instant (< 5 seconds) | 24x7x365 | ₹0 (Free) |
| **NEFT** | ₹1 | No upper limit *(₹25 Lakh online limit)* | Half-hourly batches | 24x7x365 | ₹0 (Free) |
| **RTGS** | ₹2,00,000 | No upper limit | Real-time continuous | 24x7x365 | ₹0 (Free) |

- **Beneficiary Cooling Period**: When a new beneficiary is added, a mandatory **30-minute cooling period** applies, during which maximum cumulative outward transfers are restricted to ₹50,000 to prevent account takeover exploits.

## 3. Account Statements & Certificates
- **Digital Statements**: Monthly, quarterly, and annual PDF account statements with digitally signed cryptographic signatures are available free of charge 24x7 via the Kosh web portal and Mitra, Kosh Bank's AI banker.
- **Interest Certificates**: Section 80TTA / 80TTB interest certificates and TDS Form 16A certificates are automatically generated and downloadable at the end of each financial quarter.
- **Balance Confirmation Certificate**: Issued instantly in PDF format for visa processing, auditor review, or official submissions.

## 4. Lost, Stolen or Compromised Cards
- **Emergency Blocking**: Customers can immediately freeze or hotlist debit and credit cards with zero wait time:
  1. Via Kosh Web/Mobile App toggle under "Card Controls".
  2. By chatting with Mitra, Kosh Bank's AI banker (saying *"freeze my card"*).
  3. By sending an SMS `BLOCK <Last 4 Digits>` to 56767.
- **Replacement Card**: Upon blocking, a replacement card with a new virtual number is generated immediately for digital wallet use, and physical card delivery occurs within 5 business days.
