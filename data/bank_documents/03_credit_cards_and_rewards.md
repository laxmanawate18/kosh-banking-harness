- **Airport Lounge Privileges**:

  - ₹1,00,00,000 (₹1 Crore) Air Accident Life Cover when tickets are purchased with the card.
  - ₹50,000 Emergency overseas medical hospitalization cover.
  - ₹25,000 Lost baggage delay protection.
- **Joining & Annual Fees**:
  - **First Year Fee**: **₹0 (Complimentary)** for introductory approved members.
  - **Subsequent Annual Fee**: ₹4,999 + GST; waived automatically on spending ₹5,00,000 in the preceding anniversary year.
- **Initial Credit Limit**: Pre-approved indicative limit of **₹3,50,000** (subject to income verification and bureau score).

## 2. Kosh Neo Cashback Credit Card
The **Kosh Neo Cashback Credit Card** (Product ID: `PROD-CARD-CASHBACK`) is an everyday powerhouse that converts spends into cash without redemption hurdles or merchant restrictions.

### Key Privileges & Features
- **Headline Rate / Cashback Matrix**:
  - **5.0% Instant Cashback** on preferred lifestyle partners: **Amazon, Flipkart, Swiggy, Zomato, Uber, and Myntra**.
  - **1.5% Flat Cashback** on all other retail e-commerce and offline point-of-sale merchant spends.
  - **1.0% Fuel Surcharge Waiver** across all fuel stations in India for transactions between ₹400 and ₹4,000 (up to ₹250 waiver per statement cycle).
- **Cashback Fulfillment**: Cashback is automatically calculated and **credited directly as a statement balance credit** within 3 working days of the statement generation date. No voucher redemption or catalogue conversion required.
- **No Caps & No Expiry**: Unlike competing cards that impose monthly caps, Kosh Neo provides **uncapped cashback** on all eligible category purchases.
- **Joining & Annual Fee**: **Lifetime Free (₹0)** with zero annual fee, zero joining fee, and zero condition-based renewal charges.
- **Initial Credit Limit**: Pre-approved indicative limit of **₹1,50,000**.

### Excluded Spends
Cashback is not accrued on fuel purchases (surcharge waiver applies instead), wallet load transactions, rent payments, educational institutional fee payments, or cash advances.

## 3. General Credit Card Policies & Security
- **Billing Cycle & Grace Period**: Interest-free credit period between **20 and 50 days** depending on the transaction date relative to the statement cycle.
- **Annual Percentage Rate (APR)**: 3.25% per month (39.0% annualized) on revolving balances.
- **Card Security Controls**: Cardholders can dynamically toggle contactless NFC tap-to-pay, international usage, online e-commerce limits, and ATM cash withdrawals via Kosh web and mobile dashboard.
