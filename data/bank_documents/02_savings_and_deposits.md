# Kosh Bank Savings Accounts & Fixed Deposit Products

## 1. Kosh Suvarna Premier Savings Account
The **Kosh Suvarna Premier Savings Account** (Product ID: `PROD-SAV-PREMIER`) is an elite retail savings account engineered for maximum yield, liquidity, and premium lifestyle privileges.

### Key Features & Rates
- **Headline Interest Rate**: **7.20% per annum**.
- **Interest Compounding & Payout**: Interest is calculated on a daily end-of-day product balance basis and credited **monthly** on the last calendar day directly into the account.
- **Minimum Balance Requirement**: **₹0 (Zero Balance)**. No Average Monthly Balance (AMB) or minimum maintenance balance penalties.
- **Debit Card**: Complimentary **Suvarna Metal Debit Card** (Visa Infinite) with zero issuance fee and lifetime zero annual maintenance charges.
- **Lounge Access**: Unlimited domestic airport lounge visits across India via Dreamfolks/Visa Infinite networks + 2 complimentary international airport lounge visits per calendar year.
- **Transaction Charges**: Zero fees on all domestic outward UPI, IMPS, NEFT, and RTGS transactions.
- **Insurance**: Complimentary personal accident insurance cover up to **₹50,00,000 (Rupees Fifty Lakhs)**.
- **Welcome Credit**: Instant ₹1,000 opening balance credit upon initial KYC verification.

### Monthly Interest Calculation Formula
$$\text{Daily Interest} = \frac{\text{Closing Balance} \times 7.20\%}{365}$$
Total monthly interest credited is the sum of daily interest accruals throughout that month.

- **Minimum Balance Requirement**: **₹0 (Zero Balance)** with no minimum maintenance penalty.

## 3. Kosh Vriddhi Smart FD
The **Kosh Vriddhi Smart FD** (Product ID: `PROD-FD-SMART`) offers competitive, guaranteed fixed-term returns with transparent liquidity terms.

### Interest Rate Matrix (as of September 2026)
- **7 Days to 14 Days**: 4.50% p.a.
- **15 Days to 45 Days**: 5.25% p.a.
- **46 Days to 90 Days**: 6.00% p.a.
- **91 Days to 180 Days**: 6.75% p.a.
- **181 Days to 364 Days**: 7.25% p.a.
- **1 Year to less than 2 Years**: **7.85% per annum** (Standard) | **8.35% per annum** (Senior Citizens).
- **2 Years to 3 Years**: 7.60% p.a.
- **3 Years to 5 Years (Tax Saver)**: 7.40% p.a. (Qualifies under Section 80C up to ₹1,50,000).
- **5 Years to 10 Years**: 7.20% p.a.

### Senior Citizen Concession
Individuals aged **60 years and above** receive an additional **+0.50% interest rate premium** across all deposit tenure brackets.

### Premature Withdrawal Policy
- **Liquidity Option**: Depositors may withdraw funds prior to maturity directly via the web portal or mobile app.
- **Premature Penalty Clause**: A standard penalty of **0.50% (50 basis points)** is deducted from the contracted interest rate or the rate applicable for the actual period the deposit remained with the bank, whichever is lower.
- **No Penalty on Renewal**: If funds from a broken FD are reinvested into a new FD of equal or longer tenure, the 0.50% premature penalty is waived in full.

### Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) & Form 15G / 15H
- Under Section 194A of the Income Tax Act, TDS at 10% is deducted if total interest income across all deposits exceeds **₹40,000** in a financial year (**₹50,000 for Senior Citizens**).
- Depositors whose total taxable income is below the exemption limit can submit **Form 15G** (for residents below 60 years) or **Form 15H** (for senior citizens aged 60+) digitally via Mitra (Kosh Bank's AI banker) or the web portal to prevent TDS deduction.
