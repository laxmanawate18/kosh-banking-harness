# Kosh Bank Lending & Mortgage Code

## 1. Kosh Express Personal Loans
The **Kosh Express Personal Loan** (Product ID: `PROD-LOAN-EXPRESS`) offers rapid liquidity with transparent, customer-centric repayment terms.

### Key Terms & Rates
- **Headline Interest Rate**: Starting from **10.50% per annum** on a reducing balance basis.
- **Loan Amount Range**: **₹50,000 to ₹15,00,000**.
- **Tenure Options**: Flexible repayment terms from **12 months to 60 months** (1 to 5 years).
- **Processing Fee**: Nominal flat **1.0% of the loan principal** + applicable GST.
- **Disbursal Speed**: 100% digital sanction and instant account disbursal within 4 hours post automated bank-statement and KYC verification.
- **Prepayment & Foreclosure Policy**:
  - **Lock-in Period**: 6 completed monthly installments (EMIs).
  - **Zero Foreclosure Penalty**: After 6 months, borrowers can foreclose the entire loan or make partial prepayments with **0% penalty charges**.
- **Eligibility Criteria**:
  - Minimum Age: 21 years (maximum 60 years at maturity).
  - Minimum Monthly Net Income: ₹35,000.
  - Credit Score: Minimum CIBIL score of **720** for prime pricing.

## 2. Kosh Griha Green Home Loan
The **Kosh Griha Green Home Loan** (Product ID: `PROD-LOAN-HOME`) provides competitive home financing with preferential rates for sustainable, eco-friendly residences.

### Key Terms & Rates
- **Headline Interest Rate**: Starting at **8.35% per annum** linked to the RBI External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR - Repo Linked).
- **Eco-Concession**: An exclusive **0.25% (25 basis points) interest rate reduction** for residential properties certified green by **GRIHA, IGBC (Indian Green Building Council), or EDGE**.
- **Maximum Loan Quantum**: Up to **₹5,00,00,000 (Rupees Five Crores)**.
- **Tenure**: Repayment tenures up to **30 years (360 months)**.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ceilings (in compliance with RBI Master Directions)**:
  - For loans up to ₹30 Lakhs: Up to **90% LTV**.
  - For loans between ₹30 Lakhs and ₹75 Lakhs: Up to **80% LTV**.
  - For loans above ₹75 Lakhs: Up to **75% LTV**.
- **Processing Charges**: 0.35% of loan amount, capped at a maximum of ₹10,000 + GST.
- **Zero Prepayment Charges on Floating Rate Loans**: As mandated by Reserve Bank of India circulars, floating-rate housing loans sanctioned to individual borrowers carry **zero prepayment and zero foreclosure fees** at any point in the loan lifecycle.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Resident and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs).
  - Minimum CIBIL Score: **750+**.

## 3. Fair Lending Practices & Transparency
In alignment with RBI Fair Practices Code for Lenders:
- Kosh provides a **Key Fact Statement (KFS)** to all loan applicants prior to loan execution, explicitly detailing the All-Inclusive Annual Percentage Rate (APR), schedule of all fees, monthly EMI breakdown, and recovery process.
- No penal interest is compounded; penal charges for delayed payments are strictly operational and levied as reasonable fee amounts rather than punitive interest escalation.
