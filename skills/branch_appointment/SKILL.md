# Branch appointments
## Workflow
Open start_banking_workflow("appointment"). Collect branch, preferred non-Sunday date within 90 days, time window and purpose. The time selection is a preference, not live availability. Review once and create P1-23. Tell the customer where to track branch confirmation. Do not invent a meeting link, a staff allocation or an appointment confirmation.
